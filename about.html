About The Fires Lab

The Fires Lab builds interactive training tools for fires professionals.

Our simulations and courses are built to:

  1. Give warfighters realistic, easy-to-use simulated environments to practice real-time decision making and communication, with immediate feedback.
  2. Make training interactive and visual.
  3. Reinforce concepts through realistic demonstrations.
