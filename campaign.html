Campaign Mode

Complete, deliberate targeting cycles in a defensive scenario — for students who have already mastered the basics.

Campaign mode is in development. It will run a full defensive scenario across multiple, connected targeting cycles rather than the single offensive cycle taught in Battle mode. Planned content includes:

  • An evolving scenario that changes across phases
  • A high-payoff target list that changes by phase
  • Advanced target selection standards
  • Priority intelligence requirements and specific information requirements
  • Named areas of interest and target areas of interest
  • Attack guidance matrix decisions limited by available ordnance
  • Intelligence coordination synchronization matrix
  • Fires coordination synchronization matrix
  • Running intelligence updates

Students will be able to skip Battle mode entirely and start here once Campaign mode is ready.
