2. Our Mission and the Commander's Guidance

You are a member of the Fire Support Coordination Cell of the 77th Combined Arms Brigade.

Mission: on order, the 77th Combined Arms Brigade seizes the Kordan River bridge in order to allow follow-on forces to reinforce the assault.

Brigade Commander's Targeting Guidance

“Fires must accomplish three things for me:

  1. Protect the bridge — stop the enemy engineers before they can drop it or block it.
  2. Disrupt enemy indirect fire — do not allow the enemy to use mortars on friendly units as we are forced to concentrate near the bridge.
  3. Disrupt enemy command and control — there is a significant risk the enemy will request indirect artillery fire or reinforcements when he learns friendly forces are concentrated near the bridge. We must disrupt his command and communications network to mitigate this risk.

Restriction on fires near the bridge. “No cratering munitions on or near the bridge — nothing that could damage the span, the abutments, or the approach road. Artillery and mortars are forbidden inside 300 metres of the bridge.”

3. Intelligence Brief (S-2)

Intelligence Preparation of the Operating Environment (IPOE) is complete. The enemy is likely to defend, then delay, on the southern side of the river.

Enemy Most Likely Course of Action

The enemy will take every action to block our advance to the south.

Destroy or block the bridge

The enemy is assessed to accomplish this by attempting to destroy or alter the bridge with their engineer team.

The engineer team is not on the bridge yet, and once it begins work about one hour is enough to make the bridge unusable.

Ambush the crossing

If unsuccessful, they will attempt to ambush friendly forces as we are vulnerable when crossing the bridge. We have already detected their anti-tank guided missile (ATGM) team, which is in position for this ambush.

It is not likely that the enemy engages friendly forces directly with the infantry, because our mechanized vehicles are too powerful. Instead, they will likely harass and delay friendly forces south of the bridge.

The ATGM team displaces every 48 hours, so the position we have will not stay good for long.

Fires on the choke-point

It is also highly likely that the enemy will engage us with their mortar platoon when we are at the known choke-point on or near the bridge.

His mortar platoon displaces immediately after every fire mission.

Ask for fires from a higher headquarters

Lastly, the enemy command post has the capability to request fire missions from their higher headquarters, which they may do as a last resort.

The enemy relocates his command post every 24 hours.

4. Assets Available to You

The brigade has three ways to deliver fires and four ways to find targets.

AssetTypeWhat it can do
M109A2 155 mm self-propelled howitzer battalionDeliveryLong range — reaches every target in the enemy area. Its shells crater, so it may not be used near the bridge.
105 mm M101 howitzer batteryDeliveryThe battery we hold the most rounds for. Its shells crater, so it may not be used near the bridge.
One-way attack UAS (OWA-UAS)Sensor / DeliveryA small drone that searches for a target, then flies into it. Small, very precise warhead: it destroys what it hits and little else. The only asset that may strike on or beside the bridge. Used once.
Long-range ISR UASSensorFlies deep for many hours with a powerful camera. Can watch one place continuously and report at once. Shared — must be tasked in advance.
Small UAS (sUAS)SensorA short-range camera drone. Confirms exact locations near the front and observes results. Short flight time.
Electronic signals indication (ESI)SensorDetects radio, wifi and satellite emissions and estimates where they come from. Good against a command post, which must transmit. Gives an area, not a point.
Scout teamSensorSoldiers in hidden positions. Very reliable, but see only what is in front of them.

Note the difference between the two aircraft: the long-range ISR UAS looks deep and stays a long time; the sUAS looks close and stays a short time. The OWA-UAS does both — look and strike — but only once.
